BRING YOUR BUSINESS TO THE CENTRAL COAST

Sydney's Central Coast is a region just over an hour north of Sydney CBD.

The local working population is over 150,000 of which approximately 30% commute into the City each day for work. Most of these commuters would prefer to work locally.

The Central Coast has a resident population of 346,000 people which will grow to over 400,000 in the next 25 years.

Gosford is the Capital City of the Central Coast which is presently seeing unprecedented investment. The NSW Government has made a commitment to the growth of the region and is investing almost a billion dollars in support of that growth. Additionally, the University of Newcastle is investing in Gosford with new campuses while at the same time private investors are investing substantially.

Best known for its relaxed lifestyle the Central Coast is embarking on a new era that will see a resurgence of investment as property developers and businesses looking to escape the congestion of Sydney create exciting new opportunities for those wishing to live and work within the region.

TOP REASONS TO RELOCATE OR EXPAND ON THE CENTRAL COAST

  • Low business costs in respect of land, occupancy, labour costs and productivity,
  • A large, adaptable and enthusiastic skilled and unskilled workforce,
  • Well-developed infrastructure including connection to the National Broadband Network throughout the whole region,
  • An ideal family lifestyle with excellent school and health facilities in a pristine environment unattainable in the city or comparable areas.
